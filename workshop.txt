title=Wishes
description=Ever wished for your character to magically get stronger or healthier? Well... your wish is my command!
description=
description=[code][h2] How it works: [/h2]
description=After looting around the world you may find items which will let you summon a guardian to grant your wishes.
description=Some guardians include the popular anime Dragon Ball's Shenron, the classic genie of the lamp or a few others.
description=
description=Invoking them once again without consuming all your wishes will grant you the new added set of wishes.
description=
description=The guardian may ONLY go away by consuming every single wish available.
description=[/code]
description=
description=[h2] - 🌟 Summoning Rituals 🌟 - [/h2]
description=This mod comes with a base of 4 different summoning rituals which invoke guardians who may grant your wishes.
description=To enable a ritual, you have to enable the base "Wishes" mod along with its corresponding ritual mod.
description=Currently features the first 3 working guardians, with another yet to come:
description=
description=[b]• Shenron and it's 7 dragon balls[/b]
description=
description=[b]• The genie of the lamp[/b]
description=
description=[b]• Wishing well [/b]
description=
description=• Praying (not implemented yet)
description=
description=[h2] - ⭐ Wishes ⭐ - [/h2]
description=Each ritual has its own wishes, with some that are more normal and others "unique" to their ritual. You can enable or disable any wish in the sandbox settings without affecting the other rituals!
description=
description=A few of the common wishes include:
description=[b]☆ Modifying a trait (Adding/Removing it)
description=
description=☆ Leveling up a skill until a certain level
description=
description=☆ Leveling up a skill just once
description=
description=☆ Healing all injuries
description=
description=☆ Curing all sickness
description=
description=☆ Setting your player's nutritional weight to the perfect value (80)[/b]
description=
description=[h2] - 🔧 Sandbox Options 🔧 - [/h2]
description=Each ritual comes with it's own sandbox options to determine:
description=
description=[b]+ Amount of uses for the summoning item (if it has any uses)
description=
description=+ Amount of wishes granted per summoning
description=
description=+ If a certain wish is enabled
description=
description=+ The cost related to each wish[/b]
description=
